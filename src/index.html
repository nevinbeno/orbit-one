Why leave things behind at all?

Bringing equipment home is heavy, expensive and often impossible. So missions are designed to do their work and stay. The hardware keeps teaching us even after it stops: astronauts brought back a piece of Surveyor 3 to see how metal ages in space, and mirrors left on the Moon are still used by scientists today. A machine that is silent is not always finished.
